Recently I've been introduced to this cool platform for making music with code called [Strudel](https://strudel.cc/workshop/getting-started/).

As someone with practically no music experience outside of mandatory lessons in secondary school, I've found that this tool provides music dummies like me the opportunity to create nice sounds through a language that I can actually speak, no music theory required.
